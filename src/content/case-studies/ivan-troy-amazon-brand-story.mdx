### Project overview

[Ivan Troy](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DQ3P7237) makes leather belts, shoes and bags in Italy and designs them in Los Angeles. The brand sells on Amazon.com, where its Ivo belt is listed in six colours: black, blue, coffee, cognac, green and red.

Amazon gives brand-registered sellers two tools to go beyond the plain listing. **A+ Content** replaces the text description with designed modules: images, feature callouts, tables and charts. **Brand Story** is a separate module that appears on every listing a brand owns, so the brand only has to tell its story once. Ivan Troy had neither. We built both.

### What the Brand Story does

The Brand Story sits above the product description on every Ivan Troy listing. It has three parts:

- **A brand banner** with the line "Designed in LA. Made in Italy." over a photograph of leather being cut by hand.
- **Three category cards** for Italian leather belts, men's Italian shoes and women's shoes, bags and belts, each with a short description and a link into that part of the range.
- **A brand Q&A** that answers the four questions buyers ask most: where the products are made, how to choose a belt size, how Italian shoe sizes compare with US sizes, and how to care for Italian leather.

Because it is shared, a buyer who lands on any Ivan Troy product sees the same story and the same answers.

### What each A+ page contains

Every colourway uses the same template, so the pages read as a set. From top to bottom, the Ivo Black Croco page contains:

1. **A hero with a model shot.** The colour name in large type, the leather and finish underneath, and a photograph of the belt being worn.
2. **Four numbered feature callouts** on a product photograph: the crocodile-embossed pattern, the polished silver buckle, the 35 mm dress width that fits standard suit and trouser loops, and made in Italy by hand.
3. **A "Handmade in Italy" detail** with a close-up of the embossed leather and a short note on how each belt is cut, pressed and edge-stitched.
4. **Two styling pairs**: "Sharp with a suit" and "Clean with denim", each with a photograph and one line of advice.
5. **A "One belt, six shades" range module** showing all six colours side by side, with the same leather, buckle and fit.
6. **A "Find your size" chart.** The rule is simple: order a belt two inches larger than your trouser waist, so a 34-inch waist takes a size 36. The chart lists trouser waists from 28 to 50 against belt sizes from 30 to 52.
7. **A comparison table across all six variants**, covering leather, buckle, width, sizes, where it is made and what each colour pairs with. The current colour is highlighted; the other five link to their own listings.

The Blue, Coffee, Cognac, Green and Red pages follow the same structure with their own photography and colour-specific copy.

### Why it matters for a small brand on Amazon

A small brand on Amazon competes with listings that have far bigger photography and advertising budgets. What it can control is whether the page answers the buyer's questions before they leave. The feature callouts show what the leather and buckle are. The size guide removes the most common reason for a return. The comparison table keeps a buyer who wants a different colour inside the brand's own range rather than back in search results.

Just as important, the template means the brand is not starting from scratch for each new product. A new colourway needs new photographs and a few lines of copy, not a new design.

The Ivo Black Croco listing is live with the Brand Story and A+ Content shown here. The remaining five colourways are in progress. We do not report sales figures for this project.
