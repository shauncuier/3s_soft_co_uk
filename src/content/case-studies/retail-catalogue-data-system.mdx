### System Design & Data Modeling

Retailers frequently struggle when spreadsheets become the default product database. We introduced a relational data model separating abstract products (styles) from specific physical items (SKU, colour, size, barcode).

### Key Technical Deliverables

- **Completeness Guardrails**: Configurable mandatory field rules based on product category (e.g. garments require fiber composition and wash care; homeware requires dimensions and weight).
- **Asset Association**: Automated mapping of high-resolution studio assets and lifestyle crops directly to specific colorway variants.
- **Auditable History**: Full revision tracking showing when attributes were updated and by whom before syncing to live production channels.
