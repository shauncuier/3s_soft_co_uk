### Project Overview

[3S Soft Price & Stock Sync for Dropshipzone](https://wordpress.org/plugins/3s-soft-price-stock-sync-for-dropshipzone/) is a production-grade WooCommerce extension engineered by 3s-Soft, available on the official WordPress.org plugin directory.

The plugin provides full automation between the **Dropshipzone** wholesale supplier API (`api.dropshipzone.com.au`) and WooCommerce stores operating across Australia and New Zealand. It automates catalog ingestion, two-way price and inventory synchronization, dynamic shipping zone rate calculations, and end-to-end order fulfillment with tracking telemetry.

---

### Core Engineering Features

#### 1. Delta "Fast Stock" Synchronization Engine
Updating stock for 10,000+ products using full catalog pagination is resource-intensive and leads to gateway timeouts. We engineered a dedicated delta pass that queries Dropshipzone specifically for SKUs whose stock levels changed since the previous check. This reduces server execution time from minutes to milliseconds, ensuring merchants never oversell during flash sales.

#### 2. Dynamic Pricing Engine & GST Compliance
Australian consumer law requires transparent GST pricing. The plugin includes a comprehensive pricing calculator:
- **Percentage & Fixed Markups**: Set global markups or granular category/supplier-level overrides.
- **Australian 10% GST Handling**: Dynamically add or extract GST based on supplier cost specifications.
- **Price Rounding Rules**: Round final prices to `.99`, `.95`, or the nearest whole dollar.
- **Stock Safety Buffer**: Deduct custom safety quantities to prevent supplier stockouts from affecting store orders.

#### 3. Live Shipping Rate Calculation at Checkout
Dropshipzone calculates shipping fees by SKU and destination postcode. We integrated a custom WooCommerce Shipping Method that:
- Captures the customer's Australian 4-digit postcode or New Zealand destination.
- Maps postcodes to Dropshipzone logistical zones with a 5-minute transient back-off cache to protect checkout response times.
- Supports merchant-defined handling fees and free shipping order thresholds.

#### 4. Automated Order Routing & Tracking Sync
- **One-Click Order Dispatch**: Automatically or manually submits customer orders and delivery addresses to Dropshipzone for picking, packing, and dispatch.
- **Bi-Directional Tracking Retrieval**: Scheduled background workers poll Dropshipzone for consignment tracking numbers and carrier updates, automatically writing them to WooCommerce order meta and transitioning orders to *Completed*.

#### 5. Enterprise Architecture & Fault Tolerance
- **Transient-Based Batch Locking**: Prevents overlapping cron jobs from creating race conditions on large catalogs.
- **Adaptive API Rate Limiting**: Token-bucket rate limiter enforcing Dropshipzone's limits (60 calls/minute, 600 calls/hour) with proactive back-off.
- **WooCommerce HPOS Certified**: Compatible with High-Performance Order Storage (`Automattic\WooCommerce\Internal\DataStores\Orders\CustomOrdersTableController`) for high-volume order throughput.
