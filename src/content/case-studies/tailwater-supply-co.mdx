### Project Overview

[Tailwater Supply Co.](https://tailwatersupply.com) is a US-based fly-fishing brand selling around 35 hand-tied trout fly patterns designed specifically for technical tailwaters, spring creeks, and freestone rivers across North America.

Its sales happen on **Amazon** and **Walmart**, not on its own checkout. The brand needed a home of its own: a place that tells the founder’s story, educates anglers on which fly to use and when, and sends ready buyers straight to the marketplace listings.

---

### What We Built

#### 1. Angler-First Catalog Architecture
Traditional e-commerce filters rely on generic price and color tags. For technical fly-fishing, anglers select gear based on ecological cues and river conditions. We engineered a custom taxonomy allowing customers to filter patterns by:
- **What the fly imitates** (Midge, Mayfly, Caddis, Stonefly, Terrestrial, Scud)
- **Water type** (Tailwaters, Spring Creeks, Freestone Rivers)
- **Target species** (Brown Trout, Rainbow Trout, Cutthroat, Brook Trout)

#### 2. "What’s on the Water" Seasonal Hatch Chart
An interactive, month-by-month guide on the homepage linking seasonal aquatic hatches to the exact flies that match them. Anglers can see what is emerging right now on their local water and navigate immediately to the pattern.

#### 3. Deep Product Pages
Each of the ~35 hand-tied patterns features granular specifications:
- Sizes and colors (e.g. Zebra Midge in hook sizes #16–#22 in 5 proven color variations)
- Real fishing notes and seasonal drift recommendations
- Direct links to related educational journal articles

#### 4. Dual Marketplace Buy Buttons
Every product sends the shopper to the matching **Amazon** or **Walmart** listing in a single click, eliminating shopping cart friction while preserving marketplace sales momentum.

#### 5. Brand Story and Angler Journal
Founder story highlighting decades on the river, paired with an SEO-optimised content engine answering real angler search queries ("How to fish midges in winter", "Tungsten vs brass beads") to rank organically.

#### 6. Search & Structured Data Foundations
- Descriptive titles (e.g., *"Zebra Midge Fly, Size 20, 5 Colors"*)
- Complete Open Graph social sharing tags
- `Product` and `BreadcrumbList` schema for Google search snippets
- LiteSpeed Cache page caching for instant page loads

### Recommended Next Improvements

1. **GA4 & Amazon Attribution Tracking**: Implement Google Analytics 4 click tracking on every Buy button and generate Amazon Attribution tags for all Amazon outbound links. This tracks exact referred sales and unlocks Amazon’s **Brand Referral Bonus** (typically returning ~10% of referral sales to the brand).
2. **Enhanced Product Schema**: Add `offers` (`price`, `availability`, `priceCurrency`) and `sku`/`gtin` to the JSON-LD schema so Google can show rich pricing badges in search results.
3. **Asset & SEO Hygiene**: Fill in the 12 empty image alt texts on the homepage and convert hero imagery to WebP with responsive `srcset` sizes.
4. **Localization & Catalog Sync**: Switch UK-spelled "colours" to US "colors" for American anglers and reconcile the 34 vs 35 pattern count across all copy.
