### Architecture & Approach

Corporate B2B sites often suffer from over-engineering on the backend while under-delivering on speed, security, and presentation. By employing an Astro static architecture, every page is pre-rendered at build time.

### Technical Foundations

- **Static Generation**: Zero database or server vulnerabilities, ideal for strict enterprise IT compliance.
- **Structured Data**: Schema.org Organization, Service, and BreadcrumbList markup implemented natively for enhanced search presence in technical sector queries.
- **Editorial Typography**: Disciplined typographic hierarchy with balanced line heights, purposeful contrast, and responsive layout scaling across all modern viewport sizes.
