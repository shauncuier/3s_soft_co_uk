## Failing or flagged audits
- **largest-contentful-paint** (all pages): LCP image is not discoverable from the HTML early enough (`lcp-discovery-insight`); no preload, lazy/eager mix in the hero collage.
- **aria-hidden-focus** (home): focusable link inside an `aria-hidden="true"` wrapper (`HeroVisual.astro`).
- **label-content-name-mismatch** (all pages): visible button text differs from its `aria-label` (WhatsApp buttons).
- **heading-order** (work, case study): heading levels skip.
- **network-dependency-tree-insight**: long critical request chain (CSS → fonts).

## Visual/UX findings (manual, 375px and 1440px)
- 375px: first screen of the home page is an empty grid with one floating badge; the headline is below the fold.
- 1440px: orbit badges overlap the H1 and lede.
- Six of eight case studies display a "Demo Brief" badge.
- Header logo is a blurred recolour of the brand mark.

## Targets for the redesign
≥95 in all four categories on mobile for home, a service page, work and a case study; LCP ≤2.0 s simulated; CLS 0; client JS ≤15 KB excluding analytics.
