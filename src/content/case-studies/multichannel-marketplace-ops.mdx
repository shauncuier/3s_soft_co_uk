### Operational Architecture

Multi-channel retailing requires strict discipline around metadata schemas. Amazon, eBay, and Shopify each require distinct categorization taxonomy and character length limits. 

We mapped the client's master catalogue data model to channel-specific validation rules, ensuring updates to prices, inventory, and titles propagate correctly without manual copy-pasting between portals.

### Key Workstreams

- **Attribute Mapping**: Normalisation of colourways, dimensions, power specifications, and compliance badges across diverse channel requirements.
- **Listing Health Monitoring**: Rapid response protocols for listing suppressions, pricing alert lockouts, and channel API error codes.
- **Stock Buffers**: Configured safety thresholds to protect top-selling products from unexpected stockouts during peak promotional windows.
