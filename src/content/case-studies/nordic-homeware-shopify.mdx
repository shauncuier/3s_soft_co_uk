### Project Execution

The storefront required a delicate balance between fine editorial photography and technical e-commerce performance. Rather than relying on heavy third-party app scripts that often degrade Shopify load times, we engineered native Liquid modules for collection filtering and slide-out cart previews.

### Implementation Highlights

- **Custom Section Architecture**: Modular sections enabling the client's marketing team to create tailored landing pages for seasonal drops without developer intervention.
- **UK Commerce Standards**: Optimised for British shoppers, with clear delivery countdowns, VAT-inclusive pricing display, and localised payment options.
- **Asset Pipeline**: Automated image responsive srcsets ensuring crystal-clear presentation on retina displays without excessive bandwidth consumption.
